Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

The invoice generator, budget planner and calculators run entirely in your browser. What you type is not sent to or stored on our servers.

We save one small preference, your chosen currency, in your browser's local storage so it carries across pages. You can clear it any time from your browser settings.

We may show ads served by third parties such as Google AdSense. These companies may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

  • Google uses advertising cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites.
