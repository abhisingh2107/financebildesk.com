Frequently asked questions

Is FinanceBilldesk really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use with no account, watermark or payment.

Is my financial data stored or uploaded?

No. Every calculation runs inside your browser and nothing you type is sent to a server.

Who are these tools for?

They are built for U.S. freelancers, contractors, small business owners and households who need a quick invoice, budget or loan, mortgage and savings estimate.

Which tool should I start with?

Use the invoice generator to bill a client, the budget planner to organize monthly spending, and the loan, mortgage, compound interest, savings goal, sales tax and margin calculators for quick answers.

Can I change the currency?

Yes. Pick a currency from the selector at the top of any page. U.S. dollars are the default, and your choice is remembered as you move between tools.
